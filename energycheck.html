01

The brief

Energy Check, an independent Dutch energy consultancy, needed a homepage that converts sceptical business visitors into conversations. A take-home assignment I treated as a real commission. [Vervang met jouw eigen samenvatting]

02

Research & strategy

Competitive analysis across the Dutch energy consultancy landscape shaped a persuasion framework: independence as the core promise, proof before claims, one clear action per scroll depth.

Independence is the product. Every section of the page had to prove it, not just say it.

03

Building the structure

Mid-fidelity wireframes established the narrative arc of the page: problem, promise, proof, action.

04

The design system

A five-colour palette — teal, petrol, warm neutrals and espresso — paired with Inter and Fraunces. Components documented for handoff.

The complete desktop homepage: research-led structure, calm confident visual language, ready for development.
