Designing with empathy and intention

I'm Shandor ten Hoven, a product designer who believes the best experiences are built on genuine understanding of people's needs.

Based in Amsterdam, the Netherlands

My path to product design wasn't traditional, and I think that's my strength. I approach design the way I approached hospitality—with genuine care for the people I'm serving. Every interface is an opportunity to make someone's day a little easier, every interaction a chance to show thoughtfulness.

From Hospitality to Design

Before designing digital products, I worked in hospitality. I learned to read a room, anticipate needs before they're voiced, and create experiences where people feel welcomed and understood. Those skills translate directly to UX design.

Great hospitality, like great design, is about empathy. It's about noticing the small details that make a difference. It's about creating systems that work seamlessly in the background so people can focus on what matters to them.

That service mindset shapes everything I create. I don't just design interfaces—I design moments of clarity, reduction of friction, and quiet delight.

My Approach

Empathy-driven design

I start every project by truly understanding the people who will use it. Their goals, frustrations, and context shape every design decision.

Clarity and simplicity

Complex problems don't need complex solutions. I strip away everything unnecessary to reveal elegant, intuitive experiences.

Thoughtful digital experiences

Great design anticipates needs and guides naturally. It should feel less like using software and more like having a helpful conversation.
