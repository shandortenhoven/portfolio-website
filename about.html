Hi, I'm Shandor

I'm a Product Designer based in Amsterdam, Netherlands, with a focus on creating intuitive, empathy-driven experiences that solve real problems.

My design philosophy centers on understanding the human context behind every interaction. I believe the best interfaces are the ones that feel effortless—where technology fades into the background and people can focus on what truly matters.

Before transitioning into product design, I studied psychology, which gave me a deep appreciation for human behavior and decision-making. This background shapes how I approach research, testing, and iteration—always questioning assumptions and validating with real users.

When I'm not designing, you'll find me exploring Amsterdam by bike, experimenting with new coffee brewing methods, or reading about systems thinking and behavioral economics.

What I Do

UX Research

User interviews, usability testing, competitive analysis, and synthesizing insights into actionable design decisions.

UI Design

Creating cohesive design systems, high-fidelity prototypes, and polished interfaces that balance aesthetics with usability.

Product Strategy

Collaborating with stakeholders to define product vision, prioritize features, and align design with business goals.

Prototyping

Building interactive prototypes to test concepts quickly, validate assumptions, and communicate design intent to developers.
