01

The brief

People with urgent questions struggle to find qualified professionals fast. Expert connects them for real-time guidance sessions. My job: design the full journey, from discovering an expert to leaving a session feeling helped. [Vervang met jouw eigen samenvatting]

02

Understanding the guest

Interviews and competitive analysis revealed the core anxiety: trust. People will not book a stranger without proof of expertise and a sense of control over cost and time.

People don't book expertise. They book confidence that this person can actually help them.

Affinity mapping the research findings
03

Meet Emma & Daniel

Two personas anchored every decision: Emma, who needs quick professional answers without corporate overhead, and Daniel, an expert who wants to share knowledge on his own schedule.

04

Mapping the journey

User flows for onboarding, booking and post-session follow-up made the invisible visible: every step where trust could break, and every moment to reinforce it.

The booking flow, where trust is won or lost
05

From sketch to screen

Low-fidelity wireframes tested the structure. Mid-fidelity tested the logic. High-fidelity brought it to life. Usability testing between each round kept assumptions honest.

06

Testing & refining

Usability tests exposed friction in the loading states and the after-session screen. Iterations followed the evidence, not my ego.
