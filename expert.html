Expert

Connecting people with experienced professionals for focused advice

Overview

Expert is a responsive web app designed to connect people with experienced professionals for quick, focused advice sessions. Users can browse experts across different fields, filter by expertise or availability, and book short consultations directly through the platform. The goal is to make professional insight more accessible by creating a simple, trustworthy environment where users can quickly find the right expert and schedule a conversation.

Challenge

Many people struggle to find reliable guidance when making career decisions, working on projects, or navigating unfamiliar situations. Existing platforms often feel overwhelming, unclear, or require long-term commitments. The challenge was to design an experience that makes discovering and booking expert advice feel quick, transparent, and approachable, while still maintaining credibility and professionalism.

Design Decisions

Clear navigation and hierarchy

Through user testing, I discovered that unclear close actions created confusion. By reducing typography to match the interface and adding a prominent "Return to dashboard" button, I created a homogeneous, clear way to close screens while maintaining hierarchy.

Visual clarity for availability

Initial designs didn't clearly communicate expert availability. By increasing opacity contrast and adjusting hierarchy, the "Not available" state became immediately apparent, reducing user confusion.

Engaging the wait

To enhance user engagement and reduce perceived wait time, I integrated a circular indeterminate progress indicator with motivational phrases that slide in dynamically, creating a more personal and positive loading experience.

Modern transcript design

User feedback indicated the initial layout felt "old-school." By modernizing the visual design with better spacing and hierarchy while maintaining readability, the transcript view aligns with contemporary app expectations.

Outcome

The final concept is a clean, responsive platform that allows users to easily browse experts, explore profiles, and book sessions in just a few steps. Clear filters, simple booking flows, and a calm visual design help reduce friction and support quick decision-making. Usability testing helped refine the interface, improving navigation clarity and call-to-action hierarchy, resulting in a more intuitive experience for both first-time and returning users.
