The brief

Endless scrolling through catalogues is a design failure. MoodStream flips the question: instead of “what do you want to watch?” it asks “how do you feel?” [Vervang met jouw eigen samenvatting]

Designing by feeling

The emotion-driven interface required a design system where colour, motion and type respond to mood without becoming a gimmick.

The screens
