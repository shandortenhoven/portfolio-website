Concept

MoodStream

MoodStream is an experimental web platform that reimagines media discovery through emotion. Users explore music and film based on how they want to feel — creating an immersive, mood-driven streaming experience. A spatial, mood-based concept that replaces traditional search with emotional exploration.

Overview

The idea

Most media platforms rely on search, categories, and algorithms. MoodStream explores a different interaction model: discovery through emotion. Instead of asking “what do you want to watch/listen to?”, the product asks “how do you want to feel?”.

What it enables

A spatial, mood-driven experience where each emotional “dimension” becomes an environment. Users can browse film and music together, preview atmospheres quickly, and transition between moods without traditional filtering overhead.

Design system

A modular system built around emotional gradients, layered backgrounds, and high-contrast typography.

The system supports mood-specific colorways while keeping navigation, spacing, and type consistent for clarity across different emotional states.

High-fidelity screens

Key screens that demonstrate the mood-based exploration model, from landing to selecting a dimension and browsing curated media.

Landing experience that sets tone immediately — minimal UI, strong typographic hierarchy, and mood as environment.

“Choose your dimension” interaction: emotions are the primary navigation — designed to encourage exploration over filtering.

Browsing within a mood: curated film + music tiles, quick preview patterns, and a clear “shift your frequency” transition to another emotional state.
