Concept

Moodstream

An experimental web platform that reimagines media discovery through emotion—explore music and film based on how you want to feel.

Overview

Moodstream is a spatial, mood-based streaming experience that replaces traditional search with emotional exploration. Instead of browsing categories, users explore a “mood space” to discover music and film that matches how they want to feel.
