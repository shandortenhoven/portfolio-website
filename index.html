Fashion trained my eye.

Hospitality taught me people.

From fashion and hotels to product design.

I'm a UX/UI and product designer in Amsterdam. Before design I studied fashion and spent nearly a decade leading hotel and hostel teams of 20+, from Amsterdam to Melbourne. Fashion gave me an eye for proportion and detail. Hospitality taught me to read people and to notice where a guest gets stuck.

After CareerFoundry's Product Design program I do user research, flows, interface design and design systems, and I build in HTML, CSS and JavaScript. I'm currently looking for my next product team.

Research
User and stakeholder interviews, competitive research, affinity mapping, usability testing
Design

Systems and build
